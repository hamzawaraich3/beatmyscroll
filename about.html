ABOUT THE PROJECT

FROM A SCROLL JOKE TO A MECHANICS LAB.

BeatMyScroll is an independent browser-game and benchmark project.

What BeatMyScroll is now

BeatMyScroll is a browser-native mechanics benchmark built around reaction time, flick acquisition, tracking, target switching, micro-precision and click cadence. The six tests combine into a persistent BMS Rating and shareable player profile.

The benchmark is aimed especially at people who enjoy mouse-heavy games and shooters, while remaining playable without installing a dedicated aim trainer.

Where the name came from

The project began as one deliberately ridiculous question: how fast can a person scroll before they stop? That original endurance game grew into leaderboards, speed statistics, RUSH gates, PB Ghost racing and a projected 3D corridor.

That game still exists as BeatMyScroll Classic. The broader site now treats scrolling as the original event rather than the only thing the project can measure.

Why browser benchmarks?

Browser tests are immediate: no installer, no account required for local results, and a friend can open the same link in seconds. They also have limitations. Display refresh, mouse hardware, browser scheduling and the operating system all affect results, which is why BeatMyScroll documents its methodology instead of presenting the measurements as laboratory instruments.

Profiles and competition

Results can stay entirely local, or a player can claim a short username and sync personal bests to the global BMS Rating leaderboard. Classic Scroll has separate records because scroll performance is intentionally not part of the shooter-oriented mechanics rating.

Independent and evolving

BeatMyScroll is built in public and changes frequently. The Dev Log records substantial changes, including performance work, scoring decisions and experiments that were replaced when they did not feel good enough.

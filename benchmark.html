HOW TO READ THE PERCENTILE

If your result is in the 90th percentile, it is at or above roughly 90% of the registered competitors included in that metric. Another way to say it: you are around the top 10% of that comparison group.

The comparison changes over time. As more players submit runs and existing players improve their records, the threshold for a particular percentile can move.

Average speed versus max speed

Average speed is the better endurance benchmark because it rewards maintaining pace. Max speed is a burst benchmark: it tells you how high your fastest observed peak climbed during the run. A player can rank extremely well in one and much lower in the other.

Different devices

Mouse wheels, trackpads and touchscreens emit browser input differently. BeatMyScroll normalizes that input for gameplay, but device design still matters. The benchmark therefore describes performance inside this game, not a laboratory measurement of your hardware.

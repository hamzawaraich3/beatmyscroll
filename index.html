A single flashy test should not define your mechanics. BMS Rating combines all six normalized scores, with heavier weight on flicks, reaction and tracking. Complete profiles range from Recruit through Demon.

The browser records raw test measurements. When you sync a result, the Cloudflare backend recalculates the normalized score instead of trusting a score sent by the browser.

HOW IT WORKS

SCROLL. ACCELERATE. DON'T STOP.

1

Build speed

Every wheel movement or swipe feeds the speed meter. Faster, denser input pushes your live scrolls-per-second higher and makes the background stretch from a calm grid into full hyperspace.

2

Protect the run

The inactivity bar is always draining. If the game goes about 1.5 seconds without meaningful scrolling, the run ends immediately. Speed matters, but surviving the slowdown matters too.

3

Chase a record

Your final result records raw score, average speed, maximum speed, survival time and your biggest combo. Registered players can place their best runs on the global Top 15 leaderboard.

02

SCORING

WHY FAST SCROLLING SCORES MORE

BeatMyScroll does not reward distance alone. The scoring system measures the intensity of your input and gradually increases the value of each scroll as your pace rises. A player who maintains a fast rhythm will outscore someone who covers the same distance in slow bursts.

The live speed stat is shown in scrolls per second. Average speed represents your pace across the active portion of the run, while maximum speed captures your fastest burst. There is no artificial display cap on max speed.

03

RUSH TARGETS

COMBOS ARE OPTIONAL. GREED IS NOT.

×1.2 ×1.3 ×1.5 ×2 ×4 ×5

Neon RUSH circles appear during a run and ask for a quick downward burst. Lower multipliers are forgiving and common; the biggest boosts demand much faster input inside a shorter window.

Completing a RUSH activates that multiplier for a limited time. Missing one can break your momentum, so the best players have to decide whether a high-value target is worth attacking while the inactivity timer is still counting down.

04

SPEED STAGES

THE GAME GETS WEIRDER WHEN YOU GET FASTER.

FAST

Sustained speed wakes up the first visual stage. Lines stretch, the screen brightens and the run starts feeling less like a web page and more like a tunnel.

POWER UP

A major jump from ordinary speed into a serious burst triggers a short transformation moment with impact flashes and a harder visual hit.

LOCKED IN

Hold elite speed long enough and the game changes again. The color treatment shifts while the background becomes more aggressive.

AURA MODE

Maintain extreme speed for an extended stretch and the final mode takes over: RGB effects, stronger hyperspace and maximum visual chaos until you slow down.

WORLDWIDE RECORDS

THREE LEADERBOARDS. ONE EXCUSE LESS.

After your first run you can optionally claim a username of up to 15 characters. Your best raw score, best average speed and best max speed are tracked separately, so one player can dominate endurance while another owns the fastest burst.

Only the Top 15 entries in each category are displayed. You never need an account just to play, and submitting a leaderboard name is optional.

FAQ

IMPORTANT SCIENCE

Is there actually a bottom?

Find out.

Does a mouse wheel count?

Yes. Mouse wheels, trackpads and touch swipes all feed the game.

Can I just drag the browser scrollbar?

No. The game scores actual wheel/touch input instead of page position, so jumping a scrollbar does not create a massive score.

Why does the run end so quickly?

That is the challenge. Roughly 1.5 seconds without input ends the run, forcing you to maintain rhythm instead of resting between bursts.
